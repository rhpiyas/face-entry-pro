About Project

About FaceEntryPro

FaceEntryPro is an AI-powered biometric attendance and campus intelligence system designed for educational institutions. It combines facial recognition, anti-spoofing technology, and real-time analytics to automate attendance recording while enhancing campus security.

The system performs local face recognition using edge AI, ensuring fast response times and privacy. It also provides administrators with smart video search, attendance analytics, and incident detection.
